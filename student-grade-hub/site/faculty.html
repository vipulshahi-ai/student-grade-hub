How these insights are calculated

Participation: Active ≥80% submitted; Intermediate active 50–79.9%; Low active <50%. These are adjustable teaching indicators, not permanent student traits. Performance has three levels based on average submitted marks: High ≥80, Intermediate 50–79.9, Low below 50. Students without graded submissions remain unclassified and display a dash.

Topic rankings use whole-activity scores mapped to a single topic or mixed-topic group. A multi-topic assignment is never presented as a precise score for each individual topic. Missing records count as zero for overall performance. A recorded zero is still submitted when the backend status says Submitted.

Performance levels describe current submitted work, not learning speed or fixed ability. Historic submission timing with no explicit evidence is Unknown.
